# 📅 Bookings & Payments Guide — Siddhi Farm Resort

This document consolidates all documentation related to **booking lifecycles, same-day bookings, payments (Razorpay & UPI), email invoices, and advance/partial payment architectures**.

---

## 1. Booking Lifecycle & Services

Siddhi Farm Resort offers two primary categories of bookings:

### Multi-Day Stays & Events
* **Services**: Master Bedroom, 2 BHK Villa, 4 BHK Villa, Wedding Ceremony, Engagement Ceremony, Birthday Party, Get Together.
* **Rules**: Check-out date must be strictly *after* check-in date (`checkOut > checkIn`). Minimum stay is 1 night.
* **Overlap Protection**: Bookings prevent conflicting dates for the same service when status is `pending` or `confirmed`.

### Single-Day Agro Tourism & Experiences
* **Services**: One Day Tour, Mini Water Park, One Day Tour + Mini Water Park, One Day Tour + Mini Adventure Park.
* **Rules**: Allows **same-day bookings** (`checkOut == checkIn`).
* **UI Behavior**:
  - Home Booking Panel: Guests can choose the same date for Check-in and Check-out.
  - Detail Page Booking: Guests select a "Visit Date", leaving the end date blank or matching the visit date for 1-day entry.

---

## 2. Booking Terms & Conditions Enforcement

* **Requirement**: Guests must view and accept the resort's Terms & Conditions before booking.
* **Verification**: Handled server-side (`termsAccepted === true` check in `app/api/[[...path]]/route.js`).
* **Storage**: Records capture `terms_accepted_at`, `terms_version`, and `terms_content`.
* **Confirmation Email**: Acceptance of terms is automatically confirmed to the customer via email upon booking creation.

---

## 3. Payment Processing & Gateways

### Primary: Razorpay Checkout
* **Supported Methods**: UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Netbanking, Wallets.
* **Integration Flow**:
  1. `POST /api/razorpay/order` creates an authorized Razorpay order ID.
  2. Frontend opens Razorpay modal with resort branding.
  3. `POST /api/razorpay/verify` performs HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.
  4. On successful verification, the booking is atomically marked `paid` and `confirmed`.

### Fallback: Manual UPI & QR Code
* If online gateway is unavailable, guests can pay directly using the resort's configured UPI ID / QR code (`/components/upi-payment.jsx`).
* Guests submit a payment claim with transaction reference (UTR number).
* Resort staff verify the claim in the Admin Dashboard and click **Mark Paid**.

---

## 4. Email Invoicing & Owner Notifications

When a booking payment is confirmed (via Razorpay or admin action):
1. **Customer Invoice**: Sent via Gmail with full itemized breakdown (service, dates, check-in/out times, guests, subtotal, discounts, amount paid, and Aadhaar number).
2. **Owner Reports**: Every address in `OWNER_EMAILS` receives the invoice plus an attached `.xlsx` spreadsheet generated by ExcelJS containing all bookings from the past 30 days.

## 5. Advance & Partial Payments Implementation

Siddhi Farm Resort features a dedicated **Advance Payments** engine designed for guests paying a deposit (e.g. 50%) online at booking time and clearing the balance upon arrival or before check-in.

### Key Architectural Guidelines
1. **Separation from Promotional Coupons**:
   - Advance codes are managed in a separate Admin tab (`/admin` → **Advance Codes**) so they never mix with price-cutting discount coupons.
   - An advance code splits the bill into a **deposit due today** and a **pending balance due at check-in** without reducing total revenue.
2. **Strict Single-Use (1x) & Auto-Deletion**:
   - Each advance code generated is a one-time token (e.g. `ADVANCE50`).
   - As soon as a guest completes a booking using the code, it is **automatically deleted** from the system and admin window, preventing any reuse.
3. **Smart Email Automation**:
   - When an advance booking is paid, the confirmation email displays:
     - Total stay amount.
     - Advance deposit paid.
     - **Pending balance due at check-in**.
     - A direct link & CTA button: `[ Pay Pending Balance Online → ]` linking to `/pay/[bookingId]`.
     - Embedded **Terms & Conditions / House Rules** in the email body.
4. **Balance Payment Portal (`/pay/[bookingId]`)**:
   - Guests can visit `/pay/[id]` anytime to view their remaining balance and pay it online via Razorpay or direct UPI QR code.
   - Once cleared, the booking status automatically transitions to `Paid in full ✓` and a zero-balance confirmation is generated.
5. **Admin Booking Desk Clearance**:
   - Staff can see `Advance Paid (₹X Pending)` badges on the Admin Booking Desk.
   - Staff can click **Clear balance** to manually mark the pending balance as collected at the front desk.
