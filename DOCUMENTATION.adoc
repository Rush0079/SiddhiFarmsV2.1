== 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

=== 1.1 Problem Statement
Urban families, couples, event planners, and corporate groups in Maharashtra seek refreshing countryside getaways, celebration venues (engagements, birthdays, weddings, get-togethers), and day picnic destinations. However, traditional agro-tourism resorts suffer from cumbersome manual booking, opaque pricing, disjointed payment collection, lack of short-stay (4–5 hours) flexibility, and missing instant confirmation documents (invoices, WhatsApp directions, and house rules).

*Siddhi Farm Resort* solves this by providing an ultra-fast, luxury online reservation and payment system with instant official tax receipts, automated WhatsApp delivery, advance deposit codes, and comprehensive admin controls.

=== 1.2 Target Users
* *Staycationers & Families*: Urban professionals looking for overnight stays in Master Bedrooms or 2/4 BHK Villas with swimming pool access.
* *Short-Stay / Day-Use Guests*: Travelers and couples wanting 4–5 hours of daytime relaxation and dining without booking an entire overnight stay.
* *Event Hosts*: Customers booking single-day lawn/hall venues for engagement ceremonies, birthday parties, weddings, and family get-togethers.
* *Day-Tour & School/Group Visitors*: Groups visiting for the day package and Mini Water Park.
* *Resort Operations Team & Admins*: Management tracking live reservations, marking payments, editing check-in/out timings, managing dynamic seasonal rates, and sending instant WhatsApp confirmations.

=== 1.3 Product Vision
[quote]
To be the premier digital gateway for luxury countryside farm stays and event celebrations in Maharashtra, offering seamless same-day and multi-day bookings with instant financial transparency and automated WhatsApp/email invoicing.

=== 1.5 User Flow
1. *Discovery*: Guest lands on `https://siddhifarm.com/`, reviews accommodations, photos, and reviews.
2. *Configuration*: Guest opens booking modal, selects service (`Master Bedroom`, `2 BHK Villa`, `Birthday Party`, etc.).
3. *Stay Duration & Slot Selection*:
   * For rooms: Guest selects _🌙 Full Overnight Stay_ OR _☀️ Day Use / Short Stay (4–5 Hours)_.
   * For events: Guest selects _Single-Day Celebration_ and preferred timing window.
4. *Guest Details & Compliance*: Guest enters Name, Phone, Email, 12-digit Aadhaar Number, optional Coupon/Advance code, and accepts Terms & Conditions.
5. *Instant Payment*:
   * Guest pays full/advance amount via Razorpay or UPI QR.
6. *Confirmation & Delivery*:
   * Success screen renders immediate WhatsApp share and PDF invoice links.
   * Server automatically triggers WhatsApp confirmation and Email invoice with 30-day admin XLSX reports.

=== 1.6 Success Metrics
* *Booking Conversion Rate*: > 4.5% of unique visitors initiating a reservation.
* *Instant WhatsApp Dispatch Reliability*: > 99.8% automated delivery rate within 5 seconds of payment confirmation.
* *Admin Settlement Efficiency*: < 10 seconds for front-desk staff to mark balance paid or adjust stay timings.
* *Zero Double-Bookings*: 100% calendar overlap prevention across all rooms and villa units.

=== 1.7 Out of Scope for Version 1
* Native iOS/Android mobile apps (PWA and mobile-responsive web app cover this).
* Automated multi-currency foreign exchange (INR is the primary currency).
* Direct POS hardware terminal integration.

== 2. Technical Architecture Document

=== 2.1 Recommended Tech Stack
* *Framework*: Next.js 15 (App Router, Server Components & Route Handlers).
* *Styling*: Vanilla Tailwind CSS with custom HSL luxury resort palettes (`#173d35`, `#315d4c`, `#d5b36a`).
* *Database & Auth*: Supabase PostgreSQL with PostgREST parameterized queries and Row Level Security (RLS).
* *Payment Gateways*: Razorpay Node SDK (Orders API + HMAC SHA256 cryptographic verification) & standard UPI Intent protocols.
* *Communication & Invoicing*:
  * WhatsApp Business Cloud API / Twilio / UltraMsg / Webhook gateways.
  * Nodemailer (Gmail App Passwords) for customer HTML receipts and owner Excel attachments.
  * ExcelJS for auto-generating 30-day booking workbooks.
* *Hosting*: Vercel Serverless Edge runtime with continuous deployment.

== 3. Security & Access Document

=== 3.1 Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
* *Customer Authentication*: Optional guest checkout (email/phone passwordless login) to maximize conversion speed.
* *Staff & Admin RBAC*:
  * `super_admin`: Full database authority, staff user creation/deletion, coupon creation, pricing adjustments.
  * `admin`: Booking management, stay timings editor, payment verification, WhatsApp dispatch.
  * `staff`: Read-only bookings desk and check-in assistance.
* *Zero Self-Deletion*: The primary Super Admin account (`rushi@gmail.com`) is cryptographically protected against accidental or unauthorized deletion.

=== 3.2 Security Protections Against Common Attacks

1. *SQL Injection*:
   * All database operations utilize Supabase PostgREST parameterized queries. Direct string concatenation in queries is strictly prohibited.
2. *DDoS & Rate Limiting (`lib/rate-limit.js`)*:
   * `POST /api/bookings`: Capped at 10 requests / 5 min per IP.
   * `POST /api/razorpay/order`: Capped at 15 requests / 5 min per IP.
   * `POST /api/razorpay/verify`: Capped at 20 requests / 5 min per IP.
   * `GET /api/advance-codes/validate`: Capped at 30 requests / 1 min per IP.
3. *HTTP Hardening Headers (`next.config.js`)*:
   * `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN` (Clickjacking prevention)
   * `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` (MIME sniffing prevention)
   * `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`
   * `Permissions-Policy: camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=()`
4. *Client-Tampering Immunity*:
   * Pricing is calculated server-side in `route.js`. The server queries the database pricing table and ignores any client-modified amount parameter.
5. *Atomic Payment-Commit Lifecycle*:
   * Coupons and advance codes are checked at order creation but only burned/incremented after cryptographic Razorpay payment verification (`HMAC-SHA256`) or admin manual confirmation.
